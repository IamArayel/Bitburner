This guide should apply to any bitnode, as its main focus is to get a profit as high as possible for your corp. If you are not sure what you are doing, make sure to create a backup before doing anything. Corporation won't reset if you do soft reset.

In this guide, each in-game term (like Aevum) or action to take (like Unlock it) will be bolded to make scanning easier.

Philosophy of my strategy
Because corp doesn't reset between each augmentation, it serves as :

a money printer after each reset (with 1m+/s to guarantee all TOR purchase right away)

a reputation booster in late game (around the time for the red pill)

(For the rest of this section, if you don't understand some concepts, feel free to keep reading and revisit it later)

Some certain division has a very high profit margin, so we want to have as much money as possible to create that industry and expand to each city. This generally means that

We need our starting industry to have a high margin profit, so we can get more money from private investors

With the money from the private investors, we create another industry with higher margin profit

We in turn get more money from private investors

We eventually have the industry with the highest margin profit possible to issue us dividend

Getting started (create a corp)
Once you start BN3.1, go immediately to the City Hall in Sector-12. There, you will see a button that said Create a Corporation. Click it as use the government fund. It takes a very long time for you to have your own 150b at the beginning. The name of the corp doesn't really matter.

The corp menu shows under World. I do recommend clicking Getting Started Guide and reading it, but skip the "tips/pointers" if you want.

Spending 150b
First, you need to create a new industry. Not all industries are created equal. Unless for the achievement, for the following runs, you do not need to unlock every division for max profit. The game tells you if a certain industry is recommended as starting industry, but I only recommend Agriculture (not the other two), because it is material based (vs product based), which means that you can start getting profit right away. If you opt for the other two (which are product based), you need to research products before earning any profit. Researching takes a long time and it's not worth in early game.

So, go ahead and create an industry in Agriculture.

Hiring Employee & Making Materials
Initially, you can have up to 3 employees, and you can upgrade size to recruit more (but don't do it yet!). Just hiring 3 should be enough.

There are 6 types of jobs. Assign one each to "Operations", "Engineer" and "Management". You will not see your corp making profit yet because you don't have raw materials. On the right side of the screen, it gives you an equation for the production (water + energy = plants + food). This means that, if you have what's on the left side (water + energy) in the warehouse, the employees will convert them to what's on the right side (plants + food).

But how do you know how much to buy? You will need to unlock "Smart Supply". It's something that you can unlock from the home page (click the tab with your corp name, and it's one of the options in unlocks). This allows you to buy exactly what your employees need to make the materials.

Once you bought it, you will see a Configure Smart Supply button. When you click it, make sure every toggle is On in the popup.

You will notice that, above Configure Smart Supply button, the current state is changing (that says Preparing... or Purchasing Materials... or something else). You will see the numbers next to Plants and Food become non-zeros. This means that you (or your employees) are making them.

Now you will need to sell them (the plants and food, not your employees). To do that, click Sell (0.000/0.000) next to each material. There's a popup asking you how much and how many you'd like to sell. For now, put MAX (meaning sell everything in the warehouse) and MP (meaning selling it at the current market price). You have to do this for each city.

If you are thinking about maximizing the profit by increasing the selling price a little bit, you don't need to. The energy used to fine-tuning this should be better spared for other things. The profit margin won't be that high anyways. Plus, there's a research that you can unlock later to set the best selling price automatically all the time.

After a few cycles, you should notice that your Revenue is no longer zero (on the left side).

Production Multiplier
Each industry can expand to 6 different cities. Do not invest too much in any single city in the early game. Instead, you'd want to expand to all 6 different cities, and unlock the warehouse right after creating an industry. Why? You will see a Production Multiplier under your profit, which basically means how fast you make product (and thus how fast you make money). It's the sum of the multiplier from each city, and each city has a base multiplier as 1. This means that you can get your multiplier to 6 by simply unlocking the warehouse of each city.

The other way of boosting the production multiplier is to have certain materials in your warehouse. You will see how so by clicking the ? next to Production Multiplier. For Agriculture, Real Estate has a very high impact.

You can see how it works by buying some Real Estate by clicking Buy (0.000). You can set the number to be as low as 10 to avoid buying too much and running out of money. Remember that the number you just set indicates how many you buy per second, so you will need to clear the purchase after a while (by also clicking the buy button).

Keep in mind that the boost on the multiplier is not linear, so you will get diminishing return for more Real Estate in your warehouse.

Buying some upgrades
On the home screen of corp, there are some upgrades that you can buy. For now, I only recommend upgrading Smart Factories (which increase productivity of your materials) and DreamSense (which passively increase your brand awareness) by only one level, or a few, totally up to you.

Buying Real Estate
Surprisingly, you can have negative balance in your corp funds as well. This means that you can buy Real Estate even with negative money. It's not that you don't need to pay them back - our current goal is to have a profit high enough to get more money from private investors. Because of the risk, however, I do recommend back up your data now before making any purchase in case you bought so much Real Estate that you fill up the entire storage and you don't have money to upgrade the warehouse.

To avoid overflowing the warehouse (if your storage is full, you can no longer buy or make materials), you should not buy too much Real Estate (they are expensive, too). As a reference point, having 7.6k Real Estate will take up 38 of your storage (in each city) and have the Production Multiplier at 26.26 (in total). This 7.6k number is completely arbitrary as you can't order exactly the same amount (without unlocking some research first). Set the buy rate conservatively and adjust them as you need. Keep in mind, you will have to manually do it for each city, and clear the purchase for each city as well.

If for any reason, the storage is full, upgrade the warehouse size, or upgrade Smart Storage from the home tab of corp. You can always restore the data with your backup.

Some other things to know
You can leave your corp for now to do other things in the game. There are a few things that I didn't explain in the section above, so I'll answer them here.

What do other jobs do (Business, Research & Development, and Training)?
Business (salesperson) allows you to sell your product faster. When you set the selling price higher than MP, it's understandable that fewer would be sold. Having people working in this position allows more products to be sold at a higher price.

Research & Development are basically getting you Scientific Research points, which you can use to unlock some researches. You can find this number right below Production Multiplier.

Training basically improves the stats of your employees. Stats seem to benefit the profit on the long run.

That said, all of these are not important in early stage. They are only beneficial on the long term. When the philosophy in mind, we only want to have a high profit to get more funds from private investors, these three jobs are not needed for now.

What's that "Awareness", "Popularity" and "AdVert.Inc"?
Similar to Business, it allows you to sell more materials. Again, in the early stage, we don't need them. There shouldn't be any problem selling everything you made at market price (MP). We might need to worry about them later.

What's up with employees' "Morale", "Happiness" and "Energy"?
They don't really matter. You don't need to bother manually raising them either (by Throwing Party or buying Coffee). As you'll notice, they increase when your industry is making money.

Do I need to "Switch to Manual Mode" to assign the jobs?
Not really. I'm hardly noticing any difference. We don't need to do that sort of micromanagement here.

Clicking through each tab and setting the price sounds really tedious. Where's the coding part?
You can automate things by gain access to Warehouse API and Office API via Unlocks. I don't recommend buying them right away because it requires 1TB RAM (I'm not sure if it's a bug, even though the official wiki said 0GB). No way you have that 1TB ready when you just start the BN.

At this point, my suggestion would to leave the game overnight for contracts to show up. You can use their rewards to purchase extra RAM on your home computer. But my corporation script doesn't dynamically increase the profit for me (like my hacking scripts would to dynamically find the most profitable server to hack); it simply saves me a lot of clicks to recruit persons, set price, etc.

Eventually, to complete this bitnode, tou would still need hacking exp for factions, reputation, etc., to unlock the red pill.

Once you have enough money, upgrade your home RAM to 2TB. This makes sure that you can run your corp script whenever you want. The money you get from your contract should be able to cover this.

I'll explain more about what to automate in a later section.

Corporation doesn't seem to run when you close the game
When you close the game for a while and re-open the game, you will see "bonus time" on the home page (kind of similar to BN2) based on the time you were offline. During the duration of bonus time, the cycle goes really fast.

Raising money for the next industry
The next industry we are interested in is Pharmaceutical.

At this point, you might not have enough money to create one. But that's fine, we can get money from private investors.

The money offered to you looks linear to your profit in my view. The first offer should take 150M shares in exchange for enough money to buy Pharmaceutical (and unlock every city and warehouse). The definition of enough varies by people, so again, if you are not sure, back up your save.

Create Drug
Agriculture is a "material-based" industry. Pharmaceutical is a mixture of "material-based" and "product-based" industry.

Drugs created can be sold immediately (as a material), while the other type of drug (shown as Pharmaceutical in the formula), that you can create by clicking Create Drug below Purchases & Upgrades, takes time (of researching) to have it ready to be sold, but it also has a higher profit.

Similar to what we did for "Agriculture", we hire 3 employees and assign them to "Operations", "Engineers" and "Management" in each city. Don't forget to set the selling price for Drugs.

For the Production Multiplier, unfortunately, Robots don't seem to do as much as we'd like compared to what impact Real Estate has to Agriculture, so we might as well just skip tinkering with it.

Then, we will click "Create Drug" to develop a new product. The city doesn't really matter, neither the name (if you are worrying about getting their names from the script later, the game provides you with an API to just get the list of all the product names from an industry). My names would simply be "1", "2", "3", etc. You can put the Design investment and Marketing investment as low as you want. The amount you put there will directly be deducted from your corp funds. 100M is enough. How much money you invest don't really matter much for now.

If you are curious, together they determine the ceiling price of your product, but for now, MP will always work. In the future, if you find it harder to sell, just discontinue the product and start a new one with more Design investment and Marketing investment, at which point you can use 1B.

You will now see them being queued and researched at the bottom of right side. You might want to keep an eye on them, because like we did for other materials, we also need to set their price manually when the research is done.

Once research is done
You should see a huge bump in your profit, from millions to tens of millions. You can check the private investors offer again, and you should see some bumps in their offer as well.

Unless you have enough money to buy the next industry (Healthcare, and expand to each city), it might also be a good idea to spend money on Pharmaceutical a little bit. You can upgrade the office space to 6 people and assign one person to each job for both industries you have. This allows you to start accruing research points to be used to unlock research later.

Upgrades to buy
You can also spend some money on Upgrades as well.

Smart Factories has a very immediate impact all your productivity. Even though it's additive, it's still worth if for a few levels.

Smart Storage can be more helpful once you have too many warehouses. You can buy one or two if you are running out of space.

FocusWires sounds like it has a indirect impact on productivity as well, so I'd also put a few levels there.

Project Insight, a few levels to help you get more research points.

All the other upgrades, relating to "ads", "charisma" or "sale," aren't that important for now, so we'll just skip them.

Progressing the game at this time
For this BN, you will still need your hacking level to destroy it. This means that you should still join factions, unlock augmentations, etc.

Bribing factions doesn't really worth it when you don't have enough money. I don't recommend doing it until later when you have a lot of cash in your crop funds.

End Game: "Healthcare"
I don't know if it's deliberate or not. If the creator of this game is from the United States, I'd certainly like to appreciate his humor.

The next industry you want to create is Healthcare. After you have unlocked it (and done the same thing that you did for the other two industries), you should see a huge spike in your profits. At this point, most of the corps are done. You have unlocked most of the potential of the corporation.

At the same time, however, there are still a few different things that you can explore.

Managing warehouse capacity
You might want to check the storage number periodically to make sure it's not overflowing. In each cycle, if you notice that it's hitting the limit, you should consider expanding it or just buying the upgrade to increase the global warehouse capacity. You can also experiment with Production Multiplier a little bit to see how much space certain materials can take while they positively impact the profit.

Hiring more employees
I don't have a golden ratio for how much to put for each job, but I simply split them evenly. Hiring more people doesn't increase your revenue immediately, so keep that in mind. However, it does increase the speed of researching for a new products and of the earning rates of research points immediately for those put into Research & Development.

Research
When you click on Research next to Scientific Research, it shows a pop-up with a list of options. Each button you see in the pop-up corresponds to one single research, and it can only be unlocked once.

Research is also specific to each industry. This means that the researches you unlocked in Agriculture doesn't apply to Healthcare.

Hi-Tech R&D Laboratory unlocks all the other researches. I'll go through each research to offer my personal ratings.

AutoBrew: Useless. As Morale, Happiness and Energy go up when your industry starts making profit.

AutoPartyManager: Useless. As Morale, Happiness and Energy go up when your industry starts making profit.

Automatic Drug Administration: Good. Benefits the profit on the long term

Bulk Purchasing: Useless. This can be used to help managing Production Multiplier easier, but when you have unlocked all other good researches, this industry is not the leading one to give you most profit anymore.

Drones: Great. Benefits the profit immediately, but needs a lot of points.

HRBuddy-Recruitment: Useless. Can be managed via scripting

Joywire: OK. Benefits the profit on the long term, although I don't know how these stats affect the productivity.

Market-TA.I: Great. Allows you to set the optimal selling price for everything. Can be controlled via script. You will see a button next to the sell button once this is unlocked.

Market-TA.II: Useless. I don't want to micromanage selling prices.

Overclock: Good. Benefits the profit on the long term

Self-Correcting Assemblers: Great. Benefits the profit immediately.

uPgrade: Fulcrum (seems to be available only to product-based industries): Great. Having more products to sell apparently means that you will have more profit!

uPgrade: Dashboard: OK. For me I memorized how much I spent when creating a product (it just goes from 100M to 1B to 10B, etc.), so all I need to do is to 10x the investment.

My unlocking sequence
Researches in one level means that I will unlock them in whichever order I'd like

Hi-Tech R&D Laboratory (not its sub-researches)

uPgrade: Fulcrum, uPgrade: Capacity.I

Market-TA.I, uPgrade: Capacity.II, Self-Correcting Assemblers

Drones (and its sub-researches)

Automatic Drug Administration (and its sub-researches), Overclock (and its sub-researches)

Everything else

After researches are unlocked
People working in Research & Development are not being helpful anymore. Remember to move them to other jobs.

Unlocking the corporation API
This allows you to write scripts to automate everything. Here's a few ideas of what I did in my script

Expand the storage if the storage is almost full before the selling phase

Increase the office size and assign the jobs. You can start with the office size with 6, and then increment by multiples of 3. What I do is to start with 6, and expand all the offices to 12, and then 30, 60, 90, 120, etc.

Expand to every city if an industry is unlocked (you will have to hardcode each city name)

Set the selling price as MAX/MP or use Market-TA.I. This helps you start selling products as soon as the research is done

Unlock the researches in sequence (and, again, you will probably have to hardcode it)

And no, sorry, because the API's all there, I'm not going to share any code. However, I'm happy to answer any questions.

Expanding to other industries
You can also try expanding to other industries as well, but unless you are for the achievement, you don't need to unlock other industries with starting cost less or equal to 300B.

Products not selling
Sometimes you might see the products you created earlier in the game (like Pharmaceutical) not selling, taking up your entire warehouse space. This could be because the investment you put when you created them was too low. Simply Discontinue them and create a new product with higher investment.

Upgrades & Unlocks
The only unlocks that I think worth buying are those that I just mentioned, and maybe Shady Accounting and Government Partnership (to unlock the achievement). Others help you micromanage, but at one point you are just tired of doing all the clicks, because we don't play this game to do tedious and repetitive work.

Upgrades related to sales aren't that important in my experience, but at one point, you will have too much money to burn, you might just as well upgrade everything evenly.

Other than that, I don't have any strict rules for upgrade. I just upgrade whenever I feel like it. I don't have a good algorithm for the script.

IPO, dividend, and destroying the bitnode
The reason to go public is so that you can issue dividend. You can issue dividend as part (or all) of your corp profit. The amount of how much you get from the dividend depends on how many shares you have out of all the available shares (including those you sold to private investors).

Because the more shares you have, the more dividend you will get, you can choose not to issue any shares in your IPO so you keep all of them to yourselves.

There seems to be a bug that you can issue dividend to yourself without going IPO. You can just call ns.corporation.issueDividends regardless of IPO state. I'm not sure if it's expected.

Also, remember that, early in the game, we have sold some shares to private investors. There are no ways (that I'm aware of) for me to buy them back. It's okay for me. The dividend I get paid is enough to cover my spending.

Bribing factions
At least in BN3, because all other multipliers are reduced, you can bribe the factions to increase your reputations with them. What I usually do is to bribe enough so I can have exactly 75 favors after I reset. After that, I'll just issue dividend to myself to donate. This allows me to progress the game the same way I do in other bitnodes.

In other BN, you don't necessarily need to bribe them because the multiplier of other income source might be higher.
